==Chapter 1==
{{chapter|1}}

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=1}} And they walked in the midst of the fire praising God, and blessing the Lord.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=2}} Then Azarias stood up, and prayed on this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire said,

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=3}} Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore:

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=4}} For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us: yea, true are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=5}} In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, ''even'' Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgment: for according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=6}} For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from thee.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=7}} In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed thy commandments, nor kept them, neither done as thou hast commanded us, that it might go well with us.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=8}} Wherefore all that thou hast brought upon us, and every thing that thou hast done to us, thou hast done in true judgment.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=9}} And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, most hateful forsakers ''of God'', and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=10}} And now we cannot open our mouths, we are become a shame and reproach to thy servants, and to them that worship thee.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=11}} Yet deliver us not up wholly, for thy name's sake, neither disannul thou thy covenant:

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=12}} And cause not thy mercy to depart from us, for thy beloved Abraham's sake, for thy servant Isaac's sake, and for thy holy Israel's sake;

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=13}} To whom thou hast spoken and promised, that thou wouldest multiply their seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that lieth upon the seashore.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=14}} For we, O Lord, are become less than any nation, and be kept under this day in all the world because of our sins.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=15}} Neither is there at this time prince, or prophet, or leader, or burnt offering, or sacrifice, or oblation, or incense, or place to sacrifice before thee, and to find mercy.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=16}} Nevertheless in a contrite heart and an humble spirit let us be accepted.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=17}} Like as in the burnt offerings of rams and bullocks, and like as in ten thousands of fat lambs: so let our sacrifice be in thy sight this day, and ''grant'' that we may wholly go after thee: for they shall not be confounded that put their trust in thee.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=18}} And now we follow thee with all our heart, we fear thee, and seek thy face.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=19}} Put us not to shame: but deal with us after thy lovingkindness, and according to the multitude of thy mercies.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=20}} Deliver us also according to thy marvellous works, and give glory to thy name, O Lord: and let all them that do thy servants hurt be ashamed;

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=21}} And let them be confounded in all their power and might, and let their strength be broken;

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=22}} And let them know that thou art Lord, the only God, and glorious over the whole world.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=23}} And the king's servants, that put them in, ceased not to make the oven hot with rosin, pitch, tow, and small wood;

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=24}} So that the flame streamed forth above the furnace forty and nine cubits.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=25}} And it passed through, and burned those Chaldeans it found about the furnace.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=26}} But the angel of the Lord came down into the oven together with Azarias and his fellows, and smote the flame of the fire out of the oven;

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=27}} And made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=28}} Then the three, as out of one mouth, praised, glorified, and blessed, God in the furnace, saying,

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=29}} Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=30}} And blessed is thy glorious and holy name: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=31}} Blessed art thou in the temple of thine holy glory: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=32}} Blessed art thou that beholdest the depths, and sittest upon the cherubims: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=33}} Blessed art thou on the glorious throne of thy kingdom: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=34}} Blessed art thou in the firmament of heaven: and above all to be praised and glorified for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=35}} O all ye works of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever,

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=36}} O ye heavens, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=37}} O ye angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=38}} O all ye waters that be above the heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=39}} O all ye powers of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=40}} O ye sun and moon, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=41}} O ye stars of heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=42}} O every shower and dew, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=43}} O all ye winds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=44}} O ye fire and heat, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=45}} O ye winter and summer, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=46}} O ye dews and storms of snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=47}} O ye nights and days, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=48}} O ye light and darkness, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=49}} O ye ice and cold, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=50}} O ye frost and snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=51}} O ye lightnings and clouds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=52}} O let the earth bless the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=53}} O ye mountains and little hills, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=54}} O all ye things that grow on the earth, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=55}} O ye fountains, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=56}} O ye seas and rivers, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=57}} O ye whales, and all that move in the waters, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=58}} O all ye fowls of the air, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=59}} O all ye beasts and cattle, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=60}} O ye children of men, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=61}} O Israel, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=62}} O ye priests of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=63}} O ye servants of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=64}} O ye spirits and souls of the righteous, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=65}} O ye holy and humble men of heart, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=66}} O Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever: for he hath delivered us from hell, and saved us from the hand of death, and delivered us out of the midst of the furnace and burning flame: even out of the midst of the fire hath he delivered us.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=67}} O give thanks unto the Lord, because he is gracious: for his mercy ''endureth'' for ever.

{{verse|chapter=1|verse=68}} O all ye that worship the Lord, bless the God of gods, praise him, and give him thanks: for his mercy ''endureth'' for ever.
