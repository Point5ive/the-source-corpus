The Ancient Text · Book of Enoch

The Beasts
of the Abyss

A Visual Chronicle in Seven Parts

In the age before memory, when the boundary between heaven and earth was thin, Enoch walked with God and was shown what no living man had seen — two great beasts, separated at creation, whose reunion would mean the end of all things.

I
Part One  ·  The Abyss Revealed

She Waits
in the Deep

Before Enoch ever left the earth, God showed him the ocean floor — a place where no light had ever touched. There, coiled in the coldest dark the world holds, was Leviathan. Female. Ancient. Patient. Her scales absorbed what little could reach her. She had been waiting since the first day of creation, and she would wait until the last.

II
Part Two  ·  Behemoth Rising

He Stands
at the Edge of Dawn

On the opposite end of creation, in a desert called Dundayin where no rain had ever fallen and no foot had ever walked, Behemoth stood at sunrise. His body was wrong in every proportion — built like a bull but the size of geography. His breath came out as hot steam into cold morning air. He was not waiting. He simply was. Permanent as a continent.

Image Prompt

A vast creature the size of a continent's desert stands on a cracked salt flat at sunrise. Legs like ancient stone pillars, breath visible as hot steam rising from its nostrils into cold morning air. A lone human figure 400 meters away, barely a pixel. Golden hour side lighting, dust haze, Hasselblad H6D-400C, hyper-real skin texture, every pore visible.

III
Part Three  ·  The Sky Fractures

Leviathan
Breaks the Surface

The ocean had held her for an age. Then she moved. A wall of water half a kilometer high tore upward as she breached, and the sky above her simply split — not from wind, not from storm, but from the weight of what she was. Lightning froze mid-strike around her neck. On the coastline in the distance, cities stood still. Not fleeing. Not yet. They had not understood what they were looking at.

Coastal city silhouettes far background, tiny and helpless. Salt spray frozen in air like glass beads, shot at 1/8000 shutter speed, RED Monstro 8K VV sensor, every water droplet razor sharp.

IV
Part Four  ·  Behemoth Walks

The Ground
Remembers Every Step

He did not charge. He did not roar. Behemoth simply walked, and everything men had built came apart beneath him the way paper comes apart in rain. A war photographer — had there been one — would have found no drama to capture. Only a foot the texture of ancient cracked leather lowering through dust, and buildings folding quietly behind it. The horror was that it was not angry. It was not anything.

Image Prompt

Ground-level shot between crumbling buildings as Behemoth moves through what was a city. Camera low, almost touching rubble. Its foot — ancient cracked leather over stone — fills the entire left frame. Dust billowing. Structural collapse mid-frame. Available light only, overcast sky, no dramatic color grading. Shot aesthetic like a war photographer caught something they weren't meant to.

V
Part Five  ·  The Approach

Two Ends
Moving Toward One

Seen from above — from the height angels look down at — it was two trails of ruin moving toward each other. Ocean on the left flooding coastlines. Desert on the right turning everything to scorched earth. Between them, a thin strip of what was left of the world. No chaos yet. Just two silhouettes and the terrible math of the distance closing between them.

Image Prompt

Aerial perspective from 30,000 feet. Ocean left, desert right, a burning strip of land between. Two massive silhouettes moving toward each other from opposite ends of the frame — flooded coastline on one side, scorched earth on the other. Shot like a satellite thermal image mixed with natural photography. Eerie calm, no chaos yet. Pure inevitability.

VI
Part Six  ·  The Collision

The Moment
the World Ends

God had separated them on the first day because He knew what would happen if they met. Now they met. The shockwave of water, fire, sand and torn earth moved outward in every direction at the speed of the end. The sky above them cracked open — not metaphorically, not poetically. It cracked, and light poured through the wound like it had been waiting on the other side for exactly this moment.

Image Prompt

Exact moment of first contact. A shockwave of water, fire, sand and torn earth erupts outward in every direction. The sky above has physically cracked open, light pouring through like a wound. Both creatures partially visible through carnage, scale impossible to comprehend. Motion blur on outer edges, center pin-sharp. The most violent natural-looking photograph ever taken.

VII
Part Seven  ·  Aftermath

Enoch Watches
the Silence After

When it was over, there was only smoke. Rising from flattened earth in every direction as far as anything could see. And on a small rock in the middle of all of it, one old man stood with his back to everything, his linen robe moving in the only wind left on earth. He had been shown this from the beginning. God had shown him so that someone would know. So that it would not happen in silence, without a witness.

Image Prompt

Wide open landscape, completely still. Smoke rising from flattened earth in every direction. One old man in ancient linen stands on a small rock in the foreground, back to camera, looking out at it all. His robe moves slightly in the only wind left. Shot at blue hour, 50mm lens equivalent, no filters. The way a documentary photographer would frame a war's end. Silence made visible.

End of the Vision

Enoch was taken from the earth before men had learned to fear what he had seen. The Book remains. The beasts remain. Somewhere in the deep, and somewhere in the desert, they are still waiting.
